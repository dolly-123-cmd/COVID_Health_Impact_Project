COMMUNITY HEALTH INTELLIGENCE

About Community Health Analysis

Community Health Analysis is a data-driven platform designed to analyze community-level health conditions, understand the impact of COVID-19, monitor recovery, assess mental health impact, and support AI-based community risk assessment.

About the Project

Understanding community health through data, analytics and intelligent risk assessment.

The Community Health Analysis platform focuses on understanding how health conditions can affect communities at a broader level. The system combines community health data, visualization, traditional risk scoring and machine learning-based analysis.


The platform provides a centralized environment where users can explore health indicators, compare communities, identify higher-risk areas, examine mental health impact and receive AI-based risk predictions.

Project Goal

To support early identification of community health concerns and provide understandable, data-driven insights that can help with monitoring, preparedness and preventive health planning.

The Problem and Our Solution

The Challenge

Community health information can be difficult to understand when it is distributed across different datasets and indicators. It may also be difficult to identify which communities need greater attention.

Our Solution

The platform brings important indicators into one dashboard and combines visualization, risk ranking, early warning analysis and AI prediction to provide a clearer community-level health picture.

AI-Based Health Risk Assessment

Intelligent Community Risk Analysis

The platform uses machine learning to analyze selected community health indicators and generate a predicted risk category together with a confidence value. This AI analysis complements the traditional risk assessment already available on the platform.

Risk Prediction Estimates a community risk category.
Confidence Shows the model's prediction confidence.
Model Accuracy Displays the trained model's reported accuracy.
